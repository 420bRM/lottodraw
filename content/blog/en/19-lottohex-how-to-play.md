Our [retailer map](/store-map.html) shows more than 10,000 lotto retailers across Korea. [LottoHex](/lottohex.html) splits that map into hexagon cells and lets you take cells with the retailers you actually visit. Walk around your neighbourhood, drop in at a few retailers, and cells in your colours spread across the map.

Two things first. You do not need to buy a ticket. And the chance of a 1st prize is 1 in 8,145,060 wherever you buy. LottoHex is a game about walking and collecting, not about where winners come from.

{{figure lottohex-map}}

## What kind of game is it?

Each cell is a hexagon 0.6 km from centre to corner. Opposite sides are about 1 km apart, roughly a 15-minute walk across. Counting only cells that contain a retailer, there are about 4,100 cells nationwide.

Whoever has visited the most retailers in a cell holds it. On a tie, whoever visited the cell most recently holds it. Cells no one holds are the "grey zone", shown in light grey. A single visit is enough to take a grey-zone cell.

## Getting started

1. Sign in with Google and choose a nickname. You appear in LottoHex by nickname.
2. Open [LottoHex](/lottohex.html), tap "Join" and agree to the notice.
3. Agree separately to location use (14 or older). Once you agree, the map opens with your location on.
4. Go to a retailer and, within 50 m, tap "I've been here" on its card. Once your location is verified, the visit is recorded and counts towards that cell.

{{figure lottohex-card}}

## Holding and taking cells

- If someone visits more retailers in a cell, the cell is theirs. On a tie, the most recent visitor wins.
- Win a lost cell back with "Visited again today" on the card of a retailer in that cell. Once a day per retailer.
- On a phone, the "To do" tab in the bottom sheet lists cells to defend and cells one visit away. The "Collection" tab gathers the cells you have visited.
- Each player gets a random cell skin (two colours and a pattern), which you can change for 30 points.

## Points and the quarterly ranking

- Visits verified by location: 10 points each. Once a day per retailer, up to 50 points a day.
- Cells are never cleared. At the start of each quarter (1 January, April, July and October) the Map ranking at that moment is finalised and kept.
- Quarterly rewards: 1st 1,000 · 2nd 500 · 3rd 300 · 4th–10th 100 points (operators excluded).
- Spend points on a new cell skin (30) or a 1-day pass (300). Points cannot be exchanged for cash.

## How your location is handled

Your location (latitude, longitude and accuracy) is taken once, at the moment you tap "I've been here"; the server checks that you are within 50 m of that retailer and discards the coordinates straight away. Only the verified retailer and the time are kept. The dot showing you on the map stays on your phone and is never sent to us.

Others see only cell holders' nicknames, cell skins, visit counts per cell and the Map ranking. Which retailers you visited, and when, is never shown. You can withdraw location consent at any time in My page, and the visits verified by location are then deleted. Details are in [section 8 of the terms](/terms.html#location) and [section 5 of the privacy policy](/privacy.html#location).

## Common questions

### Can I tap at home?

No. You must be within 50 m of the retailer.

### What if it says the accuracy is too low?

We do not judge when the error exceeds 100 m. Go outdoors, turn on location (GPS) and try again a moment later.

### Do I have to buy a ticket?

No. Just drop in at the retailer.

### Do closed retailers count?

No. Only retailers open now count.
