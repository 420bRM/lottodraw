Our [retailer map](/store-map.html) shows more than 10,000 lotto retailers across Korea. [LottoHex](/lottohex.html) splits that map into hexagon blocks and lets you take blocks with the retailers you actually visit. Walk around your neighbourhood, drop in at a few retailers, and blocks in your colours spread across the map.

Two things first. You do not need to buy a ticket. And the chance of a 1st prize is 1 in 8,145,060 wherever you buy. LottoHex is a game about walking and collecting, not about where winners come from.

{{figure lottohex-map}}

## What kind of game is it?

Each block is a hexagon 0.6 km from centre to corner. Opposite sides are about 1 km apart, roughly a 15-minute walk across. Counting only blocks that contain a retailer, there are about 4,100 blocks nationwide.

Whoever has visited the most retailers in a block holds it. On a tie, whoever visited the block most recently holds it. Blocks no one holds are the "grey zone", shown in light grey. A single visit is enough to take a grey-zone block.

## Getting started

1. Sign in with Google and choose a nickname. You appear in LottoHex by nickname.
2. Open [LottoHex](/lottohex.html), tap "Join" and agree to the notice.
3. Agree separately to location use (14 or older). Once you agree, the map opens with your location on.
4. Go to a retailer and, within 50 m, tap "I've been here" on its card. Once your location is verified, the visit is recorded and counts towards that block.

## Holding and taking blocks

- If someone visits more retailers in a block, the block is theirs. On a tie, the most recent visitor wins.
- Win a lost block back with "Visited again today" on the card of a retailer in that block. Once a day per retailer.
- On a phone, the "To do" tab in the bottom sheet lists blocks to defend and blocks one visit away. The "Collection" tab gathers the blocks you have visited.
- Each player gets a random block skin (two colours and a pattern), which you can change for 30 pts.

## Points and the quarterly ranking

- Visits verified by location: 10 pts each. Once a day per retailer, up to 50 pts a day.
- Block points: 0.1 pts a day for each block you hold, counted just after midnight each day from the blocks held then (operators excluded).
- Blocks are never cleared. At the start of each quarter (1 January, April, July and October) the Map ranking at that moment is finalised and kept.
- Quarterly rewards: 1st 1,000 · 2nd 500 · 3rd 300 · 4th–10th 100 pts (operators excluded).
- Spend points on a new block skin (30 pts) or a 1-day pass (300 pts). Points cannot be exchanged for cash.

## How your location is handled

Your location (latitude, longitude and accuracy) is taken once, at the moment you tap "I've been here"; the server checks that you are within 50 m of that retailer and discards the coordinates straight away. Only the verified retailer and the time are kept. The dot showing you on the map stays on your phone and is never sent to us.

Others see only block holders' nicknames, block skins, visit counts per block and the Map ranking. Which retailers you visited, and when, is never shown. You can withdraw location consent at any time in My page, and the visits verified by location are then deleted. Details are in [section 8 of the terms](/terms.html#location) and [section 5 of the privacy policy](/privacy.html#location).

## Common questions

### Can I tap at home?

No. You must be within 50 m of the retailer.

### What if it says the accuracy is too low?

We do not judge when the error exceeds 100 m. Go outdoors, turn on location (GPS) and try again a moment later.

### Do I have to buy a ticket?

No. Just drop in at the retailer.

### Do closed retailers count?

No. Only retailers open now count.
