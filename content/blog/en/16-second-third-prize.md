---
title: How much do Lotto 2nd and 3rd prizes pay? Averages of 56.95m and 1.45m KRW, worked out
description: How the 2nd and 3rd prizes are set, their averages, highs and lows over 1,158 real draws, the draw where 664 games won 2nd prize, why the amount holds steady as sales grow, and tax and where to claim.
---

The 1st prize makes the news every week, but 2nd and 3rd prizes come up far more often. These days a single draw produces around 90 winning games at 2nd prize and around 3,400 at 3rd. Neither prize is a fixed amount, so both change from draw to draw. Using the real results of all 1,158 draws from draw 88, when a game started costing 1,000 KRW, to draw 1245, here is what they actually pay.

## What separates 2nd from 3rd

| Prize | To match | Combos | Odds per game |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2nd | 5 winning numbers + bonus | 6 | 1 in 1,357,510 |
| 3rd | 5 winning numbers | 228 | 1 in 35,724 |

One bonus number makes a 38-fold difference in the odds. The 228 combinations for 3rd prize come from the 6 ways to pick five of the six winning numbers, times one of the 38 numbers left once the winning numbers and the bonus are taken out.

## How the amounts are set

Half of the ticket sales go into prizes. The fixed 4th (50,000 KRW) and 5th (5,000 KRW) prizes are paid first, and what is left is split 75% to 1st, 12.5% to 2nd and 12.5% to 3rd. Each prize's share divided by its number of winning games gives the prize per game.

Because 2nd and 3rd prize get the same 12.5%, the two pay out the same total every draw. Checking all 1,158 draws, the largest gap between the 2nd-prize total and the 3rd-prize total was 4,458 KRW — just the leftovers from cutting each prize to a whole won. The same total is shared by dozens of games at 2nd prize and thousands at 3rd, so a 3rd-prize game pays about 1/38 of a 2nd-prize game.

## What they actually paid

| Measure | 2nd prize | 3rd prize |
|---|---|---|
| Average | 56.95m KRW | 1.45m KRW |
| Median | 55.99m KRW | 1.47m KRW |
| Highest | 122,970,658 KRW (draw 255) | 1,982,242 KRW (draw 91) |
| Lowest | 6,895,880 KRW (draw 1057) | 627,634 KRW (draw 1019) |
| Winning games per draw | 54 on average | 2,031 on average |

Nine times out of ten, 2nd prize paid between 38m and 79m KRW, and 3rd prize between 1.1m and 1.7m KRW. The highs and lows came down to the number of winning games. The top 2nd prize, draw 255 (20 October 2007), had only 13 winning games, while the lowest, draw 1057 (4 March 2023), had 664.

## Sales doubled, 2nd prize did not

In 2005 sales were 51.9 bn KRW per draw; by 2025 they were 119.2 bn KRW, 2.3 times as much. Yet the average 2nd prize per game barely moved, from 56.26m to 53.76m KRW. Bigger sales do mean a bigger 2nd-prize pot, but more games sold also means proportionally more 2nd-prize wins. Per draw, 2nd-prize games went from 37.6 on average to 92.

You can check this with a little arithmetic. Of every 1,000 KRW game, 500 KRW goes to prizes, and 4th and 5th prize take about 180 KRW of that on average. 12.5% of the remaining 320 KRW or so, about 40 KRW, goes to 2nd prize. 2nd prize comes up once in 1,357,510 games, so one 2nd-prize game is worth 40 KRW × 1,357,510, or about 54m KRW. However large the sales, this calculation stays the same.

The actual average is a little higher because the numbers people choose are not spread evenly. In just over half of all draws, 2nd prize has slightly fewer winning games than the estimate and pays slightly more, while only a handful of draws, where popular numbers came up, see 2nd-prize wins pile up.

## Crowded draws and empty draws

Compare the number of 2nd-prize games estimated from sales with the actual number, and since draw 88 the two match almost exactly overall. Draw 1057, however, had 664 — eight times the estimated 83. According to reports at the time, 103 of them were bought at one shop in Seoul by the same person with the same numbers. Buying the same numbers on several tickets, or many people choosing the same numbers, leaves the odds unchanged but adds games to share with, so each game's share shrinks.

At the other extreme, some draws had no 2nd-prize winner at all: draws 3 and 5, back when a game cost 2,000 KRW. Every draw since 88 has had a 2nd prize, and even the fewest, draw 255, had 13 winning games.

## Tax: 22% on 2nd prize, nothing on 3rd

Lottery prizes of 2m KRW or less are tax-free; above that, 22% is taken from the prize minus the 1,000 KRW ticket (33% on any part above 300m KRW).

| Prize | Before tax | Tax | Take-home |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2nd, average | 56.95m KRW | 12.53m KRW | 44.42m KRW |
| 2nd, highest (draw 255) | 122.97m KRW | 27.05m KRW | 95.92m KRW |
| 3rd, average | 1.45m KRW | 0 KRW | 1.45m KRW |
| 3rd, highest (draw 91) | 1.98m KRW | 0 KRW | 1.98m KRW |

Since draw 88 not even the largest 3rd prize has gone over 2m KRW, so under today's rules 3rd prize is tax-free. Until 2022 anything over 50,000 KRW was taxed at 22% on the whole amount, so back then a 1.45m KRW 3rd prize lost about 320,000 KRW to tax.

## Where and by when to claim

2nd and 3rd prizes are paid at any NH NongHyup Bank branch (except some small outlets). Bring the winning ticket and your ID; the tax is withheld when you are paid. One year after the payout start date, unclaimed money goes to the Lottery Fund, so do not miss the deadline. The steps for a 1st prize are in [how do you actually claim a 1st prize?](/blog/how-to-claim-first-prize.html)

## Summary

- 2nd prize pays about 56.95m KRW per game on average, about 44.42m KRW after tax.
- 3rd prize pays about 1.45m KRW on average, and at 2m KRW or less it is tax-free.
- The two prizes always share the same total; the amount per game depends on the number of winning games.
- Even as sales grow, the amount per game stays almost the same.
