Lotto tickets are sold at about 9,100 retailers across Korea (October 2026, Dong-haeng Lottery's retailer search). With every one of them on our [retailer map](../store-map.html), a few places stand out for reasons other than numbers: the shops at the ends of the country, a shop alone on an island, and two 1st-prize retailers across the street from each other.

One thing first: the chance of a 1st prize is 1 in 8,145,060 wherever you buy. This is about places, not about where winners come from.

## The four ends

| Direction | Retailer | Where | Record |
|---|---|---|---|
| Southernmost | Kkaturi Moseulpo | Seogwipo, Jeju · 33.22°N | 2nd prize once |
| Northernmost | "Northernmost Lottery Retailer" | Goseong, Gangwon · 38.50°N | no 1st or 2nd yet |
| Westernmost | Baengnyeong Lotto | Baengnyeong Island, Ongjin · 124.72°E | 1st once · 2nd three times |
| Easternmost | Dajullae Store | Ulleung · 130.91°E | 2nd four times |

The northernmost shop really is called **"Northernmost Lottery Retailer"** (최북단복권판매점), in Goseong, Gangwon. The southernmost is in Moseulpo, the harbour town for boats to Marado and Gapado islands. They are about 620 km apart in a straight line. ([south on the map](../store-map.html#map=14/33.2219/126.2522/0/0) · [north](../store-map.html#map=14/38.5003/128.4242/0/0))

## The most remote: Baengnyeong Island, 151 km to the next shop

The westernmost shop, **Baengnyeong Lotto**, is also the most remote: the nearest other retailer is **151 km** away, about the distance from Seoul to Daejeon. The island is roughly four hours by ferry from Incheon, so this is the only place to buy a ticket there, and it has sold one 1st prize. ([on the map](../store-map.html#map=12/37.9722/124.7176/0/0))

## Two 1st-prize retailers 28 m apart

In Gwangjin-gu, Seoul, **Gwangnaru Lottery** and **Uri Sikpum** have both sold a 1st prize, and they are just **28 m** apart, the closest pair of 1st-prize retailers (two shops in the same building are left out). Each has one 1st prize, with 9 and 14 2nd prizes. ([on the map](../store-map.html#map=17/37.546/127.1047/0/0))

This says more about busy streets than about prizes: retailers cluster where people walk, and those places sell more. In Jung-gu, Busan, one retailer has **17** others within 500 m (one spot in Jung-gu, Seoul, has the same number).

## Rest stops and islands

- **Highway rest stops**: 16 retailers have "rest stop" (휴게소) in their name, with 26 1st prizes between them. **Mokhwa Rest Stop** in Sacheon alone has 22 1st and 125 2nd prizes, eighth in the national ranking — lots of passing traffic means lots of tickets sold.
- **Islands**: among the four island counties of Ulleung, Ongjin, Sinan and Wando, the retailer with the most 1st prizes is **Dokkaebi Bangmangi** in Wando (6 1st, 15 2nd).
